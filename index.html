One room. One lake.
Zero hurry.

Ember is a single-room guesthouse on the edge of a still mountain lake. One wall is glass. The fire hangs from the ceiling. The nearest neighbor is a forest.

Mornings start with fog on the water and coffee on the dock. Evenings end at the firepit. In between there is a sauna, a stack of records, and no reason to check the time.

The middle of nowhere.
On purpose.

Ember sits alone on the north shore, past where the road stops being paved. There is one neighbor, and it is a mountain. Phones lose a bar on the drive in and nobody has ever complained.

The water is glass in the morning and ink at night. Loons at dawn, owls after dark. Bring someone who can sit still, or someone who needs to learn.

The lake goes quiet around eight. You will too.

Everything's already up there.

The firewood is stacked, the sauna is lit with one switch, and the kitchen is stocked better than most homes. Arrive with groceries and a change of clothes. That's the whole packing list.

The lake sets the schedule.

Kayak the shore

5 min walk

Ride the lakeshore trail

10 min drive

Nineteen miles of packed gravel hugging the water, flat enough to talk the whole way.

Hike the high country

15 min drive

Alpine trailheads start where the road ends. Summits before lunch, sauna after.
